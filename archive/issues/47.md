## Executive Summary & Purpose

The citizens, park-bench scholars, and open-source contributors of Amsterdam Noord hereby issue a non-terminating Civic Command Performance to Jack Black and Kyle Gass (Tenacious D).

Flipping the feudal tradition of royal summons on its head, this petition commands "The Greatest Band in the World" to present themselves at Buikslotermeerplein for a free, public, outdoor acoustic jam and community screening. The event operates entirely in the public park space outside the corporate cinema, requiring zero indoor venue permissions or commercial ticket sales.

## Technical & Operational Execution

 * Guerrilla Projection Mapping: High-lumen, battery-powered LED projectors operated from park benches project community-submitted MP4s, open-source visual art, and tribute media directly onto the exterior facade of the cinema and surrounding buildings at night.
 * Low-Decibel Mesh Audio: Rather than high-wattage PA systems that trigger municipal noise violations, audio is broadcast via a distributed network of low-volume, synchronized Bluetooth micro-speakers spread across park benches, keeping sound at conversational decibel levels.
 * Multimodal Archiving (MP4 + Cover Art): All acoustic covers, video invitations, and visual artwork submitted to the thread are bundled into self-contained MP4 files featuring embedded cover art and deposited to Zenodo with DOI pending.
 * Crocodile Fountain Convergence: Integrates directly with attogram.003 (Restoration of the Blender Fountain Crocodile), transforming Buikslotermeerplein into a unified civic art and sound sanctuary.
