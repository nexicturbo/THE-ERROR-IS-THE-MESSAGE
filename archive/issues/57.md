The Technomage Core Lore (Babylon 5)
 * Technology as Incantation: They use microscopic tech (nanites, neural links, embedded circuits) to alter reality around them. To an outsider, raising a hand to light a fire looks like sorcery; to the Technomage, it’s just a invisible sub-routine running in the background.
 * Radical Mobility: A Technomage carries their entire operational stack on their body. They don't need a desk, a tower, or an headquarters. They walk into a room with a staff, a cloak, and hidden systems, execute an intervention, and vanish.
 * The "Apparent Magic" Rule: As Arthur C. Clarke famously wrote (and the Technomages live by): "Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic." They intentionally keep their tech hidden to provoke awe, confusion, or compliance in the people around them.
