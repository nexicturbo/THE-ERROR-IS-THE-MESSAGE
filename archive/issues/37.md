## 2. Copyright & license block

Two versions below — pick one before this leaves draft.

### Option A — Hold in David's name, assign to the stichting later (recommended)

> © 2026 [David — legal name], Amsterdam.
> Rights to be assigned to the Provo 42 Foundation upon its formal incorporation.
>
> - Code: **MIT License** © 2026 [David — legal name]
> - Creative artifacts: **CC BY 4.0** © 2026 [David — legal name]
> - House layer: **Provo 42 Artifact License** © 2026 [David — legal name]

*Rationale: the stichting does not legally exist until the notarial deed passes, so it cannot yet be the rights holder. A natural person holds the rights now and assigns them on incorporation.*

### Option B — Keep the i.o. notice as written

> © 2026 "The Provo 42 Foundation" (in formation)
> "Stichting in oprichting" → "i.o." → **"Provo 42 Foundation i.o."**
>
> - Code: **MIT License** © 2026 Provo 42 Foundation i.o.
> - Creative artifacts: **CC BY 4.0** © 2026 Provo 42 Foundation i.o.
> - House layer: **Provo 42 Artifact License** © 2026 Provo 42 Foundation i.o.

*Caveat: attributing copyright to an "i.o." entity is legally shaky since the entity isn't yet established. Use only if you accept that risk for provenance/branding reasons.*
