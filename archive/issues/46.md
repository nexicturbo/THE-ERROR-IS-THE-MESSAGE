Topic One: Soundtracks, Embedded MP4s, and Auditory Remembrance
Linking audio directly to visual album covers and MP4 video containers creates a multimodal memory anchor. In cognitive science, auditory remembrance is deeply tied to emotional retention, spatial mapping, and pattern recognition.
When you pair an MP4 video file directly with its visual cover art and full soundtrack, you establish a multi-sensory feedback loop:
 * Neurological Coupling: The visual cover art triggers instant recognition in the visual cortex, while the soundtrack activates auditory working memory.
 * Metadata Integrity: Packing audio, video, and cover art into a single self-contained MP4 container preserves the provenance log—preventing detached audio tracks or missing artwork when archived on platforms like Zenodo.
 * Protocol Application: This method provides the exact media spec for archiving audio-visual research outputs (such as bird call recordings from the Corvidae census or experimental SLM audio outputs) into permanent, citable DOIs.
